Services

Six commercial capabilities. One joined-up approach.

From the first bid decision to the final account, we cover the commercial life of a contract. Each piece of work is scoped and agreed with you up front.

01

Bid and tender pricing

A good fit when you have a significant bid in flight, a gap in the pricing team, or want a second pair of eyes before sign-off.

We build the price from the ground up, working with your operational and technical colleagues so the model reflects how the work will really be delivered.

  • Bottom-up pricing and resource builds, for services and project work
  • Pricing strategy that balances competitiveness against margin targets
  • Tender review: commercial and contractual commentary on key risks
  • Risk and opportunity registers carried into the financials
  • Support at internal sign-off, and a clean handover to mobilisation

02

Contract and commercial management

A good fit when a contract needs a firmer commercial grip, or a project needs commercial leadership through delivery.

Hands-on commercial management of live contracts and projects, from award through delivery to final account.

  • Pre-award support, contract execution and post-award management
  • Variations, change control and recovery of unbilled work
  • Extension, renewal and renegotiation
  • Subcontractor and supply-chain commercial management
  • Dispute avoidance and resolution

03

Commercial finance and modelling

A good fit when the numbers behind a deal or a business unit are not clear enough to steer by.

Finance-trained commercial support that turns data into decisions the whole business can follow.

  • P&L, cash flow and variance analysis against budget and forecast
  • Budgeting, forecasting and business partnering
  • Bespoke financial and pricing models with sensible controls and checks
  • Costing of new products, services and tenders
  • Management reporting and KPIs that people actually use

04

Risk and contract advisory

A good fit when you are about to commit to terms and want to understand what they really mean for risk and margin.

Practical commentary on contracts, written for commercial decision-makers rather than lawyers. We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice; we work alongside your legal counsel.

  • Review of key terms and risk allocation in tenders and contracts
  • NEC, JCT and public sector framework forms
  • Back-to-back terms for subcontractors and suppliers
  • Compliance with contract obligations and company policy

05

Commercial reviews

A good fit when margin is drifting, a contract is nearing renewal, or you want to know what is really driving cost to serve.

An independent review of how a contract or portfolio is performing commercially, with practical recommendations rather than a long report.

  • Margin and cost-to-serve analysis
  • Performance mechanisms, KPIs and service credits
  • Lessons learnt fed back into future pricing
  • A prioritised action plan to protect and recover value

06

Interim support and training

A good fit when you need cover for a vacancy, a peak in bid workload, or want to build skills in-house.

Experienced commercial capability for the period you need it, with knowledge passed on so it stays when we leave.

  • Interim commercial and bid management
  • Coaching for bid, commercial, finance and operational teams
  • Briefings on contract terms and commercial risk
  • Commercial processes and governance that fit how you run
  • Set-up and onboarding for MADE Pricing
