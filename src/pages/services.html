Services

Commercial support for every stage of an FM contract.

Four ways we help FM providers price well, protect margin and decide with confidence. Each piece of work is scoped and agreed with you up front.

01

Bid and tender pricing

A good fit when you have a significant bid in flight, a pricing gap in the team, or want a second pair of eyes before sign-off.

We build the price from the ground up, working with your operational and technical colleagues so the model reflects how the service will really be delivered.

  • Bottom-up pricing and resource builds for hard and soft services
  • Pricing strategy that balances competitiveness against margin targets
  • Tender review: commercial and contractual commentary on key risks
  • Risk and opportunity registers carried into the financials
  • Support at internal sign-off, and a clean handover of the model to mobilisation

02

Commercial review of live contracts

A good fit when margin is drifting, a contract is nearing extension, or you want to know what is really driving cost to serve.

An independent review of how a contract is performing commercially, with practical recommendations rather than a long report.

  • Margin, P&L and cash flow review with variance analysis
  • Change control, variations and recovery of unbilled work
  • KPI and performance-mechanism review
  • Preparation for extension, renewal and renegotiation
  • Early identification of commercial risk, and how to mitigate it

03

Pricing models and tools

A good fit when pricing lives in spreadsheets nobody fully trusts, or you want a repeatable, auditable way to price.

Models that are clear enough to defend and flexible enough to reuse, built around your rates, services and approval process.

  • Bespoke financial and resource models, with sensible controls and checks
  • Labour rate build-ups, rate libraries and indexation logic
  • Set-up and onboarding for MADE Pricing, loaded with your own rates and task library
  • Documentation your team can pick up and run with

04

Training and interim support

A good fit when you need cover for a commercial vacancy, a peak in bid workload, or want to build skills in-house.

Experienced commercial capability for the period you need it, with knowledge passed on so it stays when we leave.

  • Interim commercial management for bids and live contracts
  • Coaching for bid, commercial and operational teams
  • Briefings on contract terms and commercial risk, including NEC and JCT forms
  • Commercial processes and reporting that fit how your business runs
