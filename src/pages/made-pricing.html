MADE Pricing

Hard services bids, built from first principles.

An estimating tool for planned and reactive maintenance. Turn a site list, an asset register and a scope into an annual price, a resource build and a maintenance schedule you can stand behind.

Open MADE Pricing Sign in

What it does

A defensible price, with every figure traceable to its inputs.

01

Built from the asset up

Price planned maintenance from the asset register: task by task, visit by visit, with every figure traceable to its inputs.

02

Statutory to gold

See the price and the people needed at four service levels, from the statutory minimum to a gold standard, side by side.

03

A real resource build

Hours and FTE by trade, resident or mobile, supervision, and every cost in the labour rate laid out and editable.

04

ELI and TUPE aware

Bring in anonymised ELI data to price transferring staff at actual cost, and see redundancy and recruitment at a glance.

05

Self-delivered, subcontracted or mixed

Choose delivery by service and by site, enter real subcontractor quotes, and compare them with the tool's estimate.

06

Contract term and audit trail

Index the price through the contract, add mobilisation costs, and keep a reasoned log of every override and saved version.

How it works

From spreadsheet to bid pack in three steps.

Import

Fill in the Excel template with sites, assets and scope, then upload it. Every row is checked and reported.

Shape

Set service levels and delivery, add quotes and ELI staff, and replace placeholder rates with your own.

Price

Review the build-up, compare versions, and export a full bid pack to Excel.

Your data stays yours

Your rates, your task library, your bids.

Each company has its own users, bids, rates and task library. Nothing is shared between companies. Start from our draft task library and make it your own.

  • Placeholder rates to get started, replaced with your own pay rates and on-costs
  • Regional rate tables, including London wage floors
  • Versions you can save, compare and reason about
