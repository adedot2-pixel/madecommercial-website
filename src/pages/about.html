About

Commercial clarity, from people who have done the job.

MADE Commercial exists to give FM providers the commercial and pricing expertise that big bids and big contracts demand, in a form that is clear, practical and honest.

Our story

Why MADE Commercial.

MADE Commercial was set up by {{site.founderName}}, a commercial manager with more than a decade in facilities management and contracting. The work has spanned pricing and negotiating bids, commercial leadership of large public sector contracts, and building the financial models behind both.

Across that time the same thing kept coming up. Good FM businesses lose margin not through poor delivery but through pricing that cannot be traced, assumptions nobody wrote down, and commercial issues spotted too late.

MADE Commercial is the answer to that: senior commercial support when you need it, and tools like MADE Pricing that put the logic in the open.

How we think

Three principles.

Clarity first

If a number cannot be explained, it cannot be defended. We show the working, in plain language.

Traceable by design

Every price, override and assumption has a reason attached, so decisions can be reviewed months later.

Practical over theoretical

Advice has to work in a real bid room and on a real contract. We would rather give you a useful answer than a long report.
